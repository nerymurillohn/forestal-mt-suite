## Brand Overview

### Who We Are

Forestal Murillo Tejada S. de R.L. de C.V. (**Forestal MT**) is a Honduran family enterprise founded in 2009 by the **Murillo Tejada** family. Based in San Francisco de la Paz, Olancho, we export premium ethnobotanical products to global markets. Our foundation is built on family traditions, the sustainable management of natural resources, and respect for our ancestors.

We specialize in artisanal and wild-harvested products that carry the spirit of Honduras and reflect centuries of indigenous knowledge. Our business model includes international exports, real estate development, local commerce, agriculture, and livestock, reflecting our commitment to resilience and long-term growth.

In a market often diluted by unsubstantiated claims, **Forestal MT** provides a verifiable chain of authenticity from forest to customer. We are more than a supplier of natural ingredients; offering products whose value lies not only in their purity and potency but in the sacred, time-honored practices used to create them.

### Mission

To preserve and ethically share Honduras' ancestral ethnobotanical wisdom through pure, natural, artisanal products. We deliver verifiable wellness benefits while sustaining ecosystems, honoring indigenous knowledge holders, and safeguarding cultural heritage.

### Vision

To be the world's most trusted source for Honduras' ethnobotanical treasures, setting the benchmark for authenticity, traceability, and regenerative trade for premium brands, researchers, and conscious consumers seeking nature's true potency.

### Slogan

#### **Exporting Nature Without Borders**

More than a tagline, this slogan embodies Forestal MT's identity and international mission: to demonstrate that Honduras can excel on the global stage. It is a promise—verifiably Honduran, ethically sourced botanicals, delivered with traceable origin, export-ready quality, and deep respect for cultural heritage.

### Brand Values

- **Authenticity:** Direct sourcing from native regions guarantees a verifiable origin and unbroken chain of trust.
- **Quality & Integrity:** Artisanal methods preserve full plant potency; industrial shortcuts are never used.
- **Excellence:** International standards and on-time delivery guide every operational cycle.
- **Trust & Relationships:** Long-term partnerships rooted in honesty, reliability, and mutual growth.

### Why Choose Us

- **Direct Sourcing** – Work in native regions with community producers at origin.
- **Forest to Customer** – Transparent, traceable path with no intermediaries.
- **Global Shipping** – Door-to-door delivery worldwide with compliant packaging and paperwork.
- **Partnership** – Long-term, transparent relationships focused on shared growth.
- **Private Label** – White-label solutions backed by export-ready documentation.
