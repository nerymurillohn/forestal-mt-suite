## Logo Meaning

### Identity

The logo consists of six pine trees, positioned with symmetry and intentional hierarchy. Each tree corresponds to a founding member of the Murillo Tejada family.

- **Center Tree (Tallest):** Nery Roberto Murillo Montalván, founder and structural backbone.
- **Front Tree (Softer Tone):** Lucy Maira Tejada Flores, spiritual and emotional axis of the family.
- **Side Trees:** Jackeline Grissell Murillo Tejada and Nery Samuel Murillo Tejada, older children driving operational leadership.
- **Outer Trees:** Nery Roberto Murillo Tejada and Nery Alfredo Murillo Tejada, youngest twins representing continuity and future stewardship.

### Meaning

The six trees represent each founder and a stable, future-facing ecosystem. The pine signals permanence and regeneration. Their layout mirrors our vertically integrated flow from origin to export and reflects governance, harmony, and resilience.

### Function

The logo serves as both emblem and narrative container. Internally it reinforces shared values, identity, and origin; externally it communicates our vertically integrated model and commitment to regenerative stewardship.

### Usage Guidance

- Always start from the SVGs in `assets/logos/source/` when scaling or recoloring to preserve fidelity.
- PNGs in `assets/logos/` are the canonical raster exports; reference them in decks, pitch assets, and packaging files.
- Use the standard (uncircled) mark whenever the layout already provides comfortable padding—hero headers, brochures, web mastheads, product packaging, and any print collateral with ample whitespace.
- Use circled variants when the canvas is tight (profile photos, social avatars, stickers, wax seals, watermarks). The built-in ring ensures consistent padding even when the hosting platform crops to a square or circle.
- Prefer PNGs for finished artwork (presentations, PDFs, email signatures, mockups) where fixed pixel dimensions are required.
- Prefer SVGs for editable master files, high-resolution print, responsive web components, or when you need to recolor/re-export new sizes.
- Maintain clearspace equal to the width of one pine tree around the mark and avoid placing text or graphics inside that boundary.
- Minimum size (screen, 96 DPI): 75 px (width) x 75 px (height) for any mark variant; smaller renders blur the six-pine hierarchy.
- Minimum size (print, 300 DPI): 250 px (width) x 250 px (height) (~21 mm) baseline; scale proportionally for larger formats and specialty finishes.
- Do not recolor the mark outside the approved palette; switch to the appropriate monochrome version instead.
- For digital avatars or stamp applications, use the circled PNG variants to maintain consistent padding.
