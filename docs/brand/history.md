## History

Our story begins not in a boardroom, but in the dense, living forests of Honduras, where one man's vision would grow into a family legacy.

In the early 1990s, Nery Roberto Murillo Montalván began working in the lumber industry. Over the years, through consistent effort, he gradually acquired and managed over 5,000 hectares of woodland—assets he viewed as a responsibility for future generations.

By 2009, that promise took institutional form. The forests transitioned from personal stewardship into the foundation of Forestal Murillo Tejada S. de R.L. de C.V., a family enterprise built to endure.

Our early years were rooted in responsible timber extraction and pine resin exports. We learned the rhythms of the forest, the demands of international markets, and the delicate balance between harvest and regeneration. But when regulatory shifts and global market pressures challenged the resin trade, we adapted.

In 2018, while partnering with Miskito territorial councils in La Mosquitia on a pine resin initiative, community members gifted us two bottles of Batana Oil—a gesture of trust that reshaped our trajectory. Although the resin project later ended when the Honduran government suspended forest management plans (*Planes de Manejo Forestal*) across La Mosquitia, those samples revealed a deeper path: bridging ancient wisdom and modern wellness.

In 2020, amid the uncertainty of the COVID-19 pandemic, we reaffirmed that purpose. We restructured as a consensus-led, family-owned company—keeping ownership entirely within the Murillo Tejada family—and committed to innovation grounded in tradition.

From that moment, **Forestal MT** continued as a custodian of living tradition. We expanded into three carefully curated collections, each one a chapter in Honduras' ancestral story:

- **Batana Oil**
- **Stingless Bee Honey**
- **Traditional Herbs**

From one handmade bottle to a multi-collection export enterprise, **Forestal MT** now serves partners across North America, Central & South America, Europe, and Asia while preserving cultural integrity and a transparent forest-to-customer chain.
