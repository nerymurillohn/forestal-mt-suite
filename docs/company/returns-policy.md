## Replacement & Return Policy

### Standard Window

- Returns accepted within **45 days** of the ship date.
- Items must arrive in resellable condition with original packaging fully intact.

### Eligibility Checklist

1. Verify customer name, address, phone, email, waybill, and invoice number match the original invoice.
2. Confirm DHL Express delivered the order directly to the customer.
3. Inspect the item for wear, missing components, or misuse damage.
4. Ensure the request falls within the 45-day window (exceptions require operations approval).
5. For kits containing consumables, no more than **25%** may be used.

### Exchange Process

- Submit an exchange authorization before issuing labels.
- Reserve replacement inventory to prevent stockouts.
- Ship exchanges with DHL Express unless the customer upgrades shipping service.

### Replenishment vs. Refunds

- Replenishment is the default resolution once items pass inspection.
- Refunds require explicit authorization; payment channel determines timing (see table below).

### Refund Timelines

| Payment Method   | Refund Timeline           |
|------------------|---------------------------|
| Credit/Debit Card| 5-7 business days       |
| Store Credit     | Instant upon return scan   |
| Gift Card        | Emailed within 24 hours    |

### Exceptions

- Final sale items are labeled on product pages and receipts; **not eligible** for returns.
- Customized bundles may be returned with a **15% restocking fee**.
- International returns must prepay duties; duties reimbursed via store credit after inspection.

### Support Note

Offer a **one-time courtesy label** for first-time customers who request a return after day 45 but before day 60.
