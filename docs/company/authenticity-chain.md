## Chain of Authenticity

Every product follows these five stages:

1. **Wild Harvest** – Indigenous artisans and community partners source botanicals in native ecosystems.
2. **Traditional Processing** – Artisanal methods preserve potency without industrial shortcuts.
3. **Quality Control** – Batch testing and documentation ensure measurable consistency.
4. **Export Ready** – Complete compliance, packaging, and paperwork prepared for international shipment.
5. **Direct to You** – Delivery without intermediaries, preserving traceability from forest to customer.
