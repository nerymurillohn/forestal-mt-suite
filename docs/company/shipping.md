## Shipping Policy

### Shipping & Reach

- Global exports handled via DHL Express under DAP (Delivered At Place) terms with real-time tracking.
- Maritime (ocean) freight offered for larger volumes.
- Clients span startups, established brands, and international distributors across retail and wholesale/bulk channels.

### Logistics Route

- Orders depart the Olancho operational center, transfer to DHL Express HQ in San Pedro Sula, and fly out of Aeropuerto Ramon Villeda Morales via DHL courier services.
- Harmonized System (HS) code mapping plus electronic customs documentation must be filed inside the DHL carrier portal before tendering freight.

### DHL Packaging Requirements (2025)

- Use strong, shock-resistant cartons; double-layer packaging for heavy or fragile items.
- Seal packages with pressure-sensitive plastic tape (minimum 48 mm wide) using the "H-taping" method for security.
- Wrap each item in shock-absorbing material (bubble wrap, foam, corrugated cardboard).
- Fill remaining voids with cushioning (newspapers, air pillows, kraft paper) to prevent shifting.
- Liquids, creams, or powders require extra absorbent barriers to contain leaks.
- Apply the DHL air waybill label flat on the top panel without covering other markings.
- Keep parcels dry and mark special handling instructions (fragile, this way up, handle with care).
- Stabilize odd-shaped items so they cannot move internally.
- Every parcel must pass a shake test; no audible internal movement.

### Shipping Guidelines

- Maximum parcel weight for international shipments: **30 kg**.
- Buyer pays duties and taxes under Delivered At Place (DAP) terms.
- Provide precise goods descriptions, HS classification, and proof of value for commercial exports.
- Required customs documents: Commercial Invoice, Air Waybill, Certificate of Origin, and Export License (when applicable).
- Incomplete, vague, or inaccurate data can trigger customs delays or seizure.
- Always comply with destination-specific regulations and verify prohibited/restricted items against DHL lists before sending.
