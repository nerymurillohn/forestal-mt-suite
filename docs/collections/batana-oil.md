## **Batana Oil**

This line represents our most refined expression of ancestral oil-making, a direct inheritance from the Miskito people—the "people of beautiful hair". It embodies purity through meticulously handcrafted, unrefined oil-based products rooted in the rainforests of La Mosquitia, Honduras.

- **Tagline:** Ancestral Miskito Elixir
- **Botanical Source:** *Elaeis oleifera*
- **Sourcing:** La Mosquitia, Honduras
- **Quality Seal:** Pure & Unrefined
- **Primary Uses:** Hair, skin, and beard support
