## **Stingless Bee Honey**

A tribute to Honduran stingless bees (*Tetragonisca angustula*, tribe Meliponini), traditionally called the jimerito bee. Revered by Mayan cultures as a symbol of the sun's spirit and forest health, this honey offers exceptional enzymatic richness and deep ancestral significance.

- **Tagline:** The Mayans Heritage
- **Botanical Source:** *Tetragonisca angustula*
- **Sourcing:** Wild-harvested from log hives in native forests and regions
- **Quality Seal:** Raw & Unfiltered
- **Primary Uses:** Eye support, respiratory comfort, immune support, and culinary delicacy
