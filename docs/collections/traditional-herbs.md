## **Traditional Herbs**

A curated library of native and adapted, wild-growing plants and herbs from Central America and the Caribbean. Rooted in traditional herbalism, these botanicals embody regional wisdom and richness.

- **Tagline:** Sacred Wisdom Rooted in Nature
- **Botanical Source:** Diverse range of native and adapted plants
- **Sourcing:** Handpicked from wild landscapes, forests, riparian areas, and native regions of Honduras
- **Quality Seal:** Wildcrafted & Shade-Dried
- **Primary Uses:** Herbalism
- **Formats:** Whole, chopped, cut, cubed, or crushed
